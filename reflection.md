# Reflection Report

## Activity Description

The goal of this Career Your Adventure activity was to complete a career-aligned experience that supports my professional development. I chose to conduct a structured job and internship application process focused on software engineering roles.

My expectations were to gain practical experience with real hiring systems, understand industry requirements, and improve my professional application materials. During this activity, I updated my resume, researched companies, prepared applications, and submitted seven job and internship applications aligned with my technical background and career interests. I also documented evidence of each submission and organized my work within this repository.

## Technical and Professional Decisions

One important decision I made was to prioritize positions related to software engineering and backend development and IT, since these areas align closely with my academic coursework and long-term goals. Instead of submitting large numbers of generic applications, I focused on understanding job descriptions and ensuring my resume clearly highlighted programming projects, problem-solving experience, and technical skills.

I also decided to refine my resume to better emphasize practical experience and projects rather than listing coursework alone. This helped me present myself more professionally and understand how employers evaluate candidates.


## Contributions

This activity was completed independently. I managed the entire process including researching companies, preparing application materials, submitting applications, and organizing documentation. My individual contributions included resume improvement, application preparation, evidence collection, and reflective analysis of my progress.


## Quality Assessment

I believe my performance was strong because I completed all planned applications and gained valuable insight into the professional hiring process. I demonstrated consistent effort and maintained organized records of my work.

If I were to repeat this experience, I would start earlier and expand my approach by networking with recruiters or attending career events. I would also tailor more cover letters to increase competitiveness.



## Career Alignment

This experience moved me closer to my career goals by helping me transition from academic preparation to professional action. Applying to real positions allowed me to better understand industry expectations and identify areas for improvement.

The process strengthened my confidence, improved my professional communication skills, and prepared me for future internship and full-time opportunities in software engineering.
