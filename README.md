# Career Your Adventure (CYA)

## Overview

This repository documents my Career Your Adventure (CYA) experience. The purpose of this project is to engage in a career-aligned activity that supports my professional development while demonstrating measurable effort, reflection, and growth.

For this assignment, I completed a structured **job and internship application campaign** focused on software engineering opportunities.


## Career Goals

### Short-Term Career Goals
- Obtain a Software Engineering Internship
- Gain real-world industry experience in software development
- Strengthen professional application materials and job search skills
- Improve communication and professional presentation

### Long-Term Career Goals
- Become a Software Engineer specializing in backend or systems development
- Work on scalable software systems and collaborative engineering teams
- Continue developing strong problem-solving and programming skills
- Contribute to impactful software products and open-source communities


## Chosen Career Adventure

My selected Career Your Adventure activity was applying to multiple software engineering and technology-related positions.

This experience simulated a real professional job search process. The activity included:

- Updating and refining my resume
- Researching companies and available roles
- Preparing application materials
- Submitting job and internship applications
- Tracking application progress
- Reflecting on professional growth and career alignment


## Deliverables

This repository contains verifiable artifacts demonstrating my work:

- **Updated Resume**
  - Located in `/resume/`

- **Application Evidence**
  - Screenshots of submitted applications and confirmations
  - Located in `/applications/`

- **Reflection Report**
  - Critical analysis of decisions, performance, and professional growth
  - Located in `reflection.md`
